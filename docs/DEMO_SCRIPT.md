# Refugee Registry — 5-Minute Live Demo Script (Uganda Category-per-Tab)

> **Important Scope & Demo Disclaimers**
> - **Demo codelists are illustrative**: Values in `data/codelists/refugee/` (settlements, reception centres, food distribution points, etc.) are demo-aligned and not an official OPM/UNHCR production export.
> - **Biometrics are not implemented**: Attributes `C01`, `C02`, and `C05` are out of scope for this build.
> - **Consent is information-only**: `informed_consent_given`, `informed_consent_date`, and `partner_data_sharing_consent` record staff-entered attestations without cryptographic or e-signature enforcement.
> - **No production hardening**: This stack is configured for local demo/evaluation (`KC_HOSTNAME_STRICT=false`, HTTP on `localhost`, self-contained `start-dev` Keycloak, and `AUTH_ENABLED=false` on backend APIs behind local UI/IAM).

## 5-Minute Walkthrough

### 1. Sign In (0:00–0:30)
1. Open `http://localhost:3150/en`.
2. Click **Sign In** — you are redirected to Keycloak (`http://localhost:8280/realms/refugee/...`).
3. Log in as `staff` with `REFUGEE_DEMO_USER_PASSWORD` from `.env`.

### 2. Browse Household Register — 8 Category Tabs (0:30–2:00)
1. In the left navigation, click **Registers -> Households**.
2. Open **`HH-DEMO-0002`** (`ISINGIRO` / `NAKIVALE`, Head `RF-DEMO-0005`, `TARGETED_CASH`, `240,000 UGX` via `MOBILE_MONEY`).
3. Click through all **8 tabs** in order:
   1. **Household Composition** (`F01` `head_of_household_id`, `F03` `group_size`, `F04` `dependency_ratio`, `F05` `is_female_headed`, `F06` `is_child_headed` — `F03`–`F06` are read-only).
   2. **Case & Registration** (`A01` `functional_record_id`, `A02` `registration_date`, `A04` `entry_point`, `A05` `registration_site`, `A06` `registering_officer_id`).
   3. **Location & Settlement** (`G01` `host_district`, `G02` `settlement_location`, `G03` `zone_cluster`, `G04` `village_block`, plus `geo_hierarchy`).
   4. **Livelihoods** (`L01` `main_income_source`, `L02` `working_age_earners`).
   5. **Targeting & Eligibility** (`M01` `assistance_category`, `M02` `vulnerability_score`, `M03` `is_new_arrival` / `new_arrival_end_date`, `M04` `programmes_enrolled`, `M05` `enrolment_date` / `exit_date` / `exit_reason`).
   6. **Payment & Delivery** (`N01` `delivery_modality` / `delivery_mechanism`, `N06` `primary_recipient_id`, `N08` `food_distribution_point`, `N09` `entitlement_amount` / `entitlement_currency`).
   7. **Distribution & Transactions** (`O01` `payment_cycle`, `O03` `payment_status`, `O04` `transaction_reference` / `transaction_date`).
   8. **Record Lifecycle** (`Q01` `lifecycle_status`, `Q04` `last_updated_at` / `last_updated_by`).

### 3. Browse Refugee Register — 10 Category Tabs (2:00–3:45)
1. In the left navigation, click **Registers -> Refugees**.
2. Open **`RF-DEMO-0001`** (`Deng GARANG`, `MALE`, `SSD`, `RECOGNISED_REFUGEE`, linked to `HH-DEMO-0001`) or **`RF-DEMO-0017`** (`Simon LOKU`, `MALE`, `SSD`, `RECOGNISED_REFUGEE`).
3. Click through all **10 tabs** in order:
   1. **Personal Identity** (`B01` `last_name`, `B02` `given_name`, `B04`–`B11`, `A03` `arrival_date`).
   2. **Identity Documents** (`IdentityDocument` table: `D01` `document_type`, `document_number`, `issue_date`, `expiry_date`).
   3. **Legal Status & RSD** (`E01` `legal_status`, `E03` `rsd_application_date`, `E07` `security_screening_status` read-only).
   4. **Household Relationship** (`link_internal_record_id`, `F02` `relationship_to_group`, `F07` `presence_status`).
   5. **Specific Needs & Protection** (`SpecificNeeds` table: `I01` `need_category` / `date_identified` / `notes`, plus `I02` `unaccompanied_separated_status`).
   6. **Health & Disability** (`J01` Washington Group 6 questions, `J02` `has_serious_medical_condition`).
   7. **Targeting & Eligibility** (`M04` `programmes_enrolled`, `M05` `enrolment_date` / `exit_date` / `exit_reason`).
   8. **Distribution (Authentication)** (`O02` `auth_result`).
   9. **Consent & Data Protection** (`P01` `informed_consent_given` / `informed_consent_date`, `P02` `partner_data_sharing_consent`).
   10. **Record Lifecycle** (`Q01` `lifecycle_status`, `Q03` `duplicate_check_result`, `Q04` `last_updated_at` / `last_updated_by`).

### 4. Search & Create-and-Approve Workflow (3:45–5:00)
1. **Search**: In **Refugees**, search `GARANG` to filter the 4 South Sudanese family members (`RF-DEMO-0001`..`0004`); in **Households**, search `HH-DEMO-0001` (`YUMBE` / `BIDIBIDI`, `180,000 UGX`).
2. **Create / Change Request**: Click **New Household** (or edit an existing record's **Livelihoods** tab, e.g., updating `working_age_earners`) and submit.
3. **Approve**: Log out of `staff`, sign in as `alex.carter` (same `.env` `REFUGEE_DEMO_USER_PASSWORD`), open **My Tasks / Approvals**, and approve the pending request. Verify the updated record in the register.

## 5 Likely Demo Questions & Honest Answers

1. **Q: Where are biometrics (`C01` fingerprints, `C02` iris, `C05` facial template)?**
   **A:** Out of scope in this build. Only the 55 non-biometric Mandatory attributes from the Uganda specification are implemented; `O02` (`auth_result`) records the outcome code (`FINGERPRINT_OK`, `IRIS_OK`, `MANUAL_OVERRIDE`, `FAILED`) without a live biometric SDK.
2. **Q: Why are `DisplacementHistory`, `Relative`, `Address`, `EducationRecord`, and `OccupationSkill` in the database but not in the UI?**
   **A:** Those 5 sub-registers had no Mandatory attributes in the Uganda specification. Their PostgreSQL tables and ORM classes are preserved so OpenG2P platform conventions do not break, while their tabs are hidden from `g2p_register_ui_tabs`.
3. **Q: Are the Uganda settlements, reception centres, and food distribution points official master data?**
   **A:** No. The geographic hierarchy (`l0`–`l3`) is loaded from the upstream `OpenG2P/openg2p-data` `ETH` pack, and `data/codelists/refugee/` provides illustrative Uganda-aligned demo codelists. Replace them with official OPM/UNHCR master data before any pilot.
4. **Q: How are `HH-` and `RF-` IDs generated?**
   **A:** By the dedicated `id-generator` service (`docker/config/id-generator.yaml`): 10-digit numeric IDs for `household` (`HH-`) and 12-digit numeric IDs for `refugee` (`RF-`).
5. **Q: Is this ready for production deployment?**
   **A:** No. It is a single-host Docker Compose demo build (`start-dev` Keycloak, HTTP `localhost`, no TLS/ingress, and permissive backend API auth settings). Production requires TLS, hardened secrets management, proper network segmentation, and enabling backend JWT/IAM enforcement on all API services.
