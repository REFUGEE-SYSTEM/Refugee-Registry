# Refugee Registry — 5-Minute Live Demo Script (Uganda Category-per-Tab)

> **Important Scope & Demo Disclaimers**
> - **Demo codelists are illustrative**: Values in `data/codelists/refugee/` are demo-aligned and not an official OPM/UNHCR production export.
> - **Biometrics are not implemented**: Attributes `C01`, `C02`, and `C05` are out of scope for this build (a standard `Photo` upload bound to `record_image_document_id` is available in **Personal Identity**).
> - **Consent is information-only**: `informed_consent_given`, `informed_consent_date`, and `partner_data_sharing_consent` record staff-entered attestations without cryptographic or e-signature enforcement.
> - **No production hardening**: This stack is configured for local demo/evaluation (`KC_HOSTNAME_STRICT=false`, HTTP on `localhost`, self-contained `start-dev` Keycloak, and `AUTH_ENABLED=false` on backend APIs behind local UI/IAM).

## 5-Minute Walkthrough

### 1. Sign In (0:00–0:30)
1. Open `http://localhost:3150/en`.
2. Click **Sign In** — you are redirected to Keycloak (`http://localhost:8280/realms/refugee/...`).
3. Log in as `staff` with `REFUGEE_DEMO_USER_PASSWORD` from `.env`.

### 2. Browse Refugee Register — 9 Category Tabs (0:30–2:30)
1. In the left navigation, click **Registers -> Refugees**.
2. Open **`RF-DEMO-0001`** (**`Deng GARANG`**, `MALE`, `SSD`, `RECOGNISED_REFUGEE`) or **`RF-DEMO-0012`** (**`Claudine NDUWIMANA`**, `FEMALE`, `BDI`, `RECOGNISED_REFUGEE`, with specific needs).
3. Click through all **9 tabs** in order:
   1. **Personal Identity** (opens first): `B01` `last_name`, `B02` `given_name`, `B04`–`B11`, `A03` `arrival_date`, and **Photo** (`record_image_document_id`).
   2. **Identity Documents** (`IdentityDocument` table: `D01` `document_type`, `document_number`, `issue_date`, `expiry_date`).
   3. **Legal Status & RSD** (`E01` `legal_status`, `E03` `rsd_application_date`, `E07` `security_screening_status` read-only).
   4. **Specific Needs & Protection** (`SpecificNeeds` table: `I01` `need_category` / `date_identified` / `notes`, plus `I02` `unaccompanied_separated_status`).
   5. **Health & Disability** (`J01` Washington Group 6 questions, `J02` `has_serious_medical_condition`).
   6. **Targeting & Eligibility** (`M04` `programmes_enrolled`, `M05` `enrolment_date` / `exit_date` / `exit_reason`).
   7. **Distribution (Authentication)** (`O02` `auth_result`).
   8. **Consent & Data Protection** (`P01` `informed_consent_given` / `informed_consent_date`, `P02` `partner_data_sharing_consent`).
   9. **Record Lifecycle** (`Q01` `lifecycle_status`, `F07` `presence_status`, `Q03` `duplicate_check_result`, `Q04` `last_updated_at` / `last_updated_by`).

### 3. Search, Photo Upload & Create-and-Approve Workflow (2:30–5:00)
1. **Search**: In **Refugees**, search `GARANG` to filter the 4 South Sudanese records (`RF-DEMO-0001`..`0004`: `Deng GARANG`, `Nyabol GARANG`, `Akech GARANG`, `Lual GARANG`).
2. **Create with Photo**: Click **New Refugee** (`/en/intake-form/refugee/new/...`). Verify **Personal Identity** is the first section and open by default. Fill in the required fields, upload an image in **Photo**, and submit.
3. **Approve**: Log out of `staff`, sign in as `alex.carter` (same `.env` `REFUGEE_DEMO_USER_PASSWORD`), open **My Tasks / Approvals**, and approve the pending request. Verify the new `RF-` record and photo appear in the register.

## 5 Likely Demo Questions & Honest Answers

1. **Q: Where are biometrics (`C01` fingerprints, `C02` iris, `C05` facial template)?**
   **A:** Out of scope in this build. Only the non-biometric Mandatory attributes from the Uganda specification are implemented; `O02` (`auth_result`) records the outcome code (`FINGERPRINT_OK`, `IRIS_OK`, `MANUAL_OVERRIDE`, `FAILED`) without a live biometric SDK. A standard **Photo** upload (`record_image_document_id`) is included in **Personal Identity**.
2. **Q: Why are `DisplacementHistory`, `Relative`, `Address`, `EducationRecord`, and `OccupationSkill` in the database but not in the UI?**
   **A:** Those 5 sub-registers had no Mandatory attributes in the Uganda specification. Their PostgreSQL tables and ORM classes are preserved so OpenG2P platform conventions do not break, while their tabs are hidden from `g2p_register_ui_tabs`.
3. **Q: Are the codelists official master data?**
   **A:** No. `data/codelists/refugee/` provides illustrative Uganda-aligned demo codelists. Replace them with official OPM/UNHCR master data before any pilot.
4. **Q: How are `RF-` IDs generated?**
   **A:** By the dedicated `id-generator` service (`docker/config/id-generator.yaml`): 12-digit numeric IDs for `refugee` (`RF-`).
5. **Q: Is this ready for production deployment?**
   **A:** No. It is a single-host Docker Compose demo build (`start-dev` Keycloak, HTTP `localhost`, no TLS/ingress, and permissive backend API auth settings). Production requires TLS, hardened secrets management, proper network segmentation, and enabling backend JWT/IAM enforcement on all API services.
